# Hydroponic for Black Thumbs: plugin for Claude

One skill, `hydroponic-tower-coach`. It asks a few questions about your tower, water and nutrients, creates a personal dashboard, and then advises you from your own readings.

## What you get

- A dashboard that says what to do today: readings, doses for your tank, drain countdown, a map of your planting sites.
- A coach that reads your readings and explains each correction, so you learn to run the tower yourself.

## How to use it

After installing, tell Claude:

> Set up my hydroponic tower dashboard.

Later, press "Copy summary for Claude" on the dashboard, paste it into a conversation and ask your question.

## Limits

- Default doses are for Terra Aquatica TriPart on soft tap water, for leafy greens and herbs. With other nutrients the skill starts low and corrects from your EC measurements.
- Claude cannot see your plants. Send a photo when something looks wrong.
- The dashboard interface is in English.

Installation instructions are in the repository's main README.
