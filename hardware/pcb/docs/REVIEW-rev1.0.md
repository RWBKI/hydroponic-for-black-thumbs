# Design review: controller board rev 1.0 (superseded by rev 1.1)

> **This is the review of rev 1.0, kept for the record. The files in this repository are rev 1.1; see [REVIEW-rev1.1.md](REVIEW-rev1.1.md) and [CHANGES.md](../CHANGES.md).**
>
> This review was produced by a separate AI session (Claude) that had not seen the board being designed and was asked to find mistakes. It is not a review by a human electronics engineer, and nothing in it has been measured on hardware. It was carried out on the files as they stood before the publication cleanup, which changed only names, title blocks and silkscreen text (see finding 21). Reference designators, net names and coordinates are unchanged.

Review date: 2026-11-15. Reviewed from the files only: `kicad/*.kicad_sch`, `kicad/*.kicad_pcb`, `kicad/*.kicad_pro`, the two custom footprints, the JLCPCB BOM and placement CSVs, and the readme. Nothing in the source files was modified; all work was done on a scratch copy.

## Verdict

I would not fabricate this board as is, although I found no error that stops it from powering up, being programmed over USB, or switching the six 12 V outputs. The schematic and the layout agree pin for pin (384 pads, 0 differences), every IC pin-out I checked against a datasheet is right, and the power, USB, ESP32 and MOSFET blocks are sound. The problems are concentrated in the part that is hardest to fix after fabrication, the pH measurement. The "guard ring" is not a ring in copper: ground pour sits 0.30 mm from the high-impedance node on both layers. The pH reference is held at 1.65 V against a ground that the EC electrodes share through the same liquid, with nothing but firmware discipline to keep DC out of the reference junction. Separately, the 3.3 V regulator is run below its specified minimum input voltage. Two of these three need a copper change, so the cheap moment to fix them is before the first order. If the only goal of a first batch is to validate the digital and pump side, the board as drawn should do that.

Findings by severity: **0 Blocker, 3 Major, 9 Minor, 11 Note.**

## How the review was done

- Netlist exported with `kicad-cli sch export netlist` (140 components, 84 named nets plus 17 intentionally unconnected pins) and parsed into a per-component pin list.
- `kicad-cli` 7.0.11 has no `sch erc` or `pcb drc` subcommand. DRC was run with `pcbnew.WriteDRCReport()` (same engine, 7.0.11), once on the board as saved and once after refilling the zones. ERC could not be run; see "Could not verify".
- Schematic-to-board comparison, track-width bottleneck search, copper spacing measurements and BOM/placement cross-checks were scripted with the `pcbnew` Python module. Copper was also plotted and inspected visually.
- Datasheets and LCSC pages that were opened are linked where they are used. Values I did not look up are marked as assumptions.
- Copper weight is not stated in the files; 1 oz (35 µm) is assumed for all resistance and current estimates.

### Supporting detail for the Major findings

**Finding 1.** The guard net choice is right: `PH_SIG` is the buffer output and follows the input. The implementation is the problem. The zone outline is too tight for copper to fit between the routed node and the outline on the north side (0.35 mm zone clearance plus 0.25 mm minimum width), and the outline stops 0.3 mm short of enclosing J3 pad 1 on the west side and U6 pad 5 on the east side.

**Finding 2.** With both EC pins floating and nothing else grounding the liquid, the circuit is workable: the liquid then sits at 1.65 V and only pin leakage flows. The finding is that this condition depends on firmware and on the installation, and that neither the readme nor the silkscreen states it.

**Finding 3.** The calculation uses 0.3 to 0.6 V for the Schottky drop. 0.6 V is the listed 1 A figure; the lower end is my estimate for 0.1 to 0.4 A, because I could not open a forward-voltage curve.

## Checked and found correct

Schematic versus layout
- Netlist comparison: 384 schematic pins against 384 numbered pads, 0 net differences; all 17 unconnected pins match; values and footprints match for all 140 footprints.
- No single-pin named net exists, so no label typo has silently split a connection.
- DRC after zone refill: 0 errors, 0 unconnected pads, 7 silkscreen-clipped-by-edge warnings (as the readme says).

Readme pin map (every entry verified against the netlist)
- Outputs 1..6 = GPIO16, 17, 26, 27, 13, 12. DS18B20 GPIO4. Floats GPIO32, 33. EC GPIO25, 14 and AIN2. pH AIN0 = shield, AIN1 = probe. Button GPIO34. LED GPIO2. 12 V sense GPIO35. I2C GPIO21 (SDA), 22 (SCL). B.Cu silkscreen legend agrees.

12 V input
- Q1 (P-channel) has drain to J1 pin 1 and source to `VIN12`, so the body diode conducts forward and blocks reverse. Gate to GND through R1 100 k; D1 BZT52C10 cathode on source, anode on gate.
- Gate-source voltage: AO3401A limit is ±12 V. D1 (9.5 to 10.5 V, [LCSC C19077408](https://lcsc.com/product-detail/Zener-Diodes_hongjiacheng-BZT52C10_C19077408.html)) clamps it to about 10 V for any input above 10 V, including a brick's 12 to 13 V open-circuit voltage; zener current at 12.6 V is 26 µA. Without D1 the rating would be exceeded, so D1 must not be omitted.
- Reverse connection: gate and source both near 0 V, FET off, 12 V across a 30 V part. The TVS is behind the FET and is not reverse-biased.
- TVS D2 cathode on `VIN12`; 24.4 V clamp is below the 30 V limits of the TPS54331 and AO3400A and the 50 V capacitors.
- Track widths: J1 to Q1 1.5 mm; Q1 to each pump terminal at least 1.2 mm (F.Cu) and 2.0 mm (B.Cu bus); terminal to MOSFET drain at least 1.0 mm. IPC-2221 external-layer estimates for a 10 °C rise: 1.0 mm 2.4 A, 1.2 mm 2.7 A, 1.5 mm 3.2 A.

Buck converter (TPS54331)
- Pin-out matches the datasheet (1 BOOT, 2 VIN, 3 EN, 4 SS, 5 VSENSE, 6 COMP, 7 GND, 8 PH). EN left floating is the documented way to enable.
- Output 5.06 V nominal. Bootstrap C6 100 nF between BOOT and PH as specified. Soft start C7 10 nF gives 4 ms (allowed 1 to 10 ms, at most 27 nF).
- Catch diode D3 SS34, cathode on `SW`, anode on GND. Inductor 6.8 µH, the value in TI's example.
- Input C4 10 µF 50 V X5R ([C13585](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C13585.html)) + C5 100 nF, 4.2 mm and 2.1 mm from pin 2. Output 3 x 22 µF 25 V X5R ([C12891](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C12891.html)).
- Feedback is taken from the output-capacitor side of the inductor.

3.3 V regulator and power OR-ing
- AMS1117 pin-out (1 GND, 2 VOUT, 3 VIN, tab VOUT) matches the symbol and the SOT-223 "tab = pin 2" footprint.
- D4 and D5 have anodes on `+5V` and `VBUS`, cathodes on `V_LDO`: USB cannot back-feed the buck output or the 5 V terminal, and the buck cannot back-feed the USB host.
- Dissipation is about 0.3 W at an assumed 0.2 A average load.

ESP32-WROOM-32E
- Symbol and footprint pad numbering match the datasheet pin table (pins 17 to 22 and 32 not connected).
- EN: R12 10 k to 3.3 V and C19 1 µF to GND, the values Espressif recommends; SW1 to GND.
- Strapping pins: GPIO0 has R13 10 k pull-up, the boot switch and no capacitor. GPIO2 drives only R15 1 k + LED to GND (low at reset). GPIO12 has R43 10 k to GND through R37 (the other five gates use 100 k), so the flash voltage strap stays low. GPIO5 and GPIO15 go only to J17.
- Input-only pins: GPIO34 has external pull-up R14 10 k and C20; GPIO35 is driven by the divider; GPIO36 and 39 go to J17 only.
- No ADC2 pin is used as an analog input. The only ESP32 analog input is GPIO35 (ADC1).
- GPIO16 and 17 are free on this module variant (no PSRAM).
- Antenna: module at the top board edge; the footprint's rule area (x 98..146, y up to 46.24) removes copper, tracks and vias on both layers, 15 mm either side of the module. Verified in the filled copper.
- Decoupling present: C17 22 µF and C18 100 nF within 6 mm of pin 2 (but see finding 4 for how they are connected).

USB and auto-reset
- CC1 and CC2 each have 5.1 k to GND. Both D+ pads and both D- pads are joined and go to CH340C pins 5 and 6.
- CH340C pin-out matches the datasheet; VCC and V3 both on 3.3 V as required for 3.3 V operation; pin 7 left open as required; TXD to RXD0 and RXD to TXD0.
- Auto-reset: Q2 base from DTR via R10, emitter RTS, collector EN; Q3 base from RTS via R11, emitter DTR, collector GPIO0. This is the standard cross-coupled network.

MOSFET outputs
- AO3400A: 48 mΩ max at VGS = 2.5 V, threshold 0.65 to 1.45 V, 30 V, 5.7 A ([datasheet](https://www.aosmd.com/res/datasheets/AO3400A.pdf)), so 3.3 V drive is adequate. Gate series 100 Ω.
- Each gate has a pull-down on the gate side, and the driving GPIOs are inputs or pulled down at reset, so outputs are off during boot and programming.
- Flyback diodes SS34: anode on the drain / terminal pin 2, cathode on `VIN12`. Correct orientation.
- Indicator LEDs: 4.7 k from `VIN12`, cathode to the drain; about 1.9 mA with the listed 2.6 to 3.1 V green LED.
- Silkscreen "+" is at terminal pin 1 (`VIN12`) and "-" at pin 2 for all six outputs; same check passed for the 12 V input and the 5 V output.

pH front end and ADC
- MCP6002: pin-out matches; input bias ±1 pA typical at 25 °C (19 pA at 85 °C, no maximum given); common-mode range VSS - 0.3 V to VDD + 0.3 V; output within 25 mV of the rails ([datasheet](https://ece-classes.usc.edu/ee459/library/datasheets/MCP6004.pdf)). With the reference at 1.65 V and the electrode at ±0.41 V around it, inputs and outputs stay between 1.24 and 2.06 V.
- Reference: R18/R19 100 k/100 k with C23 1 µF, buffered by U6A. Probe buffer U6B at unity gain. 1 pA into 500 MΩ is 0.5 mV, under 0.01 pH.
- The ADC measures AIN0 - AIN1 = reference minus probe, so the sign is inverted relative to the electrode voltage; firmware must account for it. Because AIN0 senses the shield itself, the ADC's 710 kΩ differential input impedance on the ±0.512 V range causes no gain error through R21.
- ADS1115: pin-out matches; ADDR to GND gives 0x48; all inputs stay within GND to VDD; AIN0 - AIN1 differential and AIN2 single-ended are both valid multiplexer settings. I2C pull-ups R16, R17 4.7 k to 3.3 V.
- B.Cu ground under U5 and U6 is continuous apart from the guard zone.

Other inputs
- DS18B20: 3.3 V through R50 47 Ω on J5.1, data on J5.2 with R26 4.7 k pull-up and R27 100 Ω series to GPIO4, ground on J6.
- Float switches: R28/R30 10 k pull-ups, 1 k + 100 nF filters into GPIO32 and 33.
- 12 V sense: R2 100 k / R3 15 k with C3 100 nF. Pin voltage 1.57 V at 12 V, 1.96 V at 15 V, 3.26 V at 25 V, always below the 3.3 V rail.

BOM and placement files

- Every BOM line's LCSC number and footprint equal the schematic's fields. Placement coordinates equal the board's pad-centre positions for all parts.
