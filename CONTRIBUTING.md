# Contributing

Contributions are welcome. This is a hobby project shared as-is; replies may take a while.

## Most wanted

1. **Schematic review.** The custom PCB has never been fabricated. If you are an electronics engineer, open an issue titled "Schematic review" with anything you find. See [docs/pcb.md](docs/pcb.md) for what has and has not been checked.
2. **Build reports.** If you wire the module version, report what matched the [wiring guide](docs/wiring.md) and what did not.
3. **Other water and nutrients.** Doses here fit one water supply and one nutrient line. Measured values for other setups are useful: tap pH and EC, nutrient brand, mL/L, resulting EC.
4. **Corrections.** Horticultural or electrical errors, unclear steps, broken links.

## How

- Open an issue before a large change.
- Keep pull requests focused on one thing.
- State whether what you are adding has been tested on real hardware or a real tower. Untested is fine if it says so.
- Documentation is in English.

## Privacy

Please do not put personal data in issues, commits or files: no real names you do not want public, addresses, order numbers or screenshots of shopping carts. Strip location metadata from photos before uploading.

## Licence of contributions

By contributing you agree that your contribution is released under the licence that covers the part you change: MIT for code, CERN-OHL-P v2 for hardware, CC BY 4.0 for documentation and photos.
