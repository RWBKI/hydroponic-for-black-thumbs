# Dashboard

One self-contained HTML file. Open `index.html` in a browser.

- Starts with demo data. Press "Start fresh" to use it for your own tower.
- Enter pH, EC, water temperature and tank level. The "Today" card lists what to do, in order of urgency.
- Works in litres or US gallons, °C or °F, and mS/cm or ppm.
- Default doses are for Terra Aquatica TriPart on soft tap water. Edit them from your own EC measurements.
- Data stays in your browser. Use "Export backup" to keep a copy or move to another device.
- "Copy summary for Claude" puts your tower's status on the clipboard, ready to paste into a conversation.

The same file is bundled in the Claude plugin, which fills in your settings and can keep your data in your Claude account instead of the browser.

Fonts load from Google Fonts when online; the page falls back to system fonts offline.
