## If you order from outside the EU

Since 1 July 2026 the EU applies a flat €3 customs duty to parcels worth €150 or less, charged per item category in the parcel rather than per parcel. A basket of many different cheap modules is hit hardest: a €3 board can cost several times its price once duty, VAT and handling are added.

Before ordering:

- Compare the landed price with EU-based sellers, item by item. Small modules often remain cheaper imported; bulky items such as enclosures and tubing often do not.
- Group identical items; they count as one category.
- Check the import charges line at checkout before paying.

Source: [European Commission, €3 customs duty for low-value parcels](https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/ensuring-fairness-and-safety-eur3-customs-duty-low-value-parcels-2026-06-29_en).
